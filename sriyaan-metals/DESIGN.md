# Sriyaan Metals design system

**Direction:** Precision in motion — premium editorial-industrial, not generic SaaS.

## Palette
- Ink `#071117`, Carbon `#0B1920`, Steel `#102832`
- Paper `#F4F7F7`, Fog `#DFE8E9`
- Teal `#0EA5B2` for trust, information and focus
- Orange `#FF8A00` for primary conversion actions only
- Muted `#88A0A9`

## Typography
Archivo Variable for condensed-feeling technical display headlines; Manrope Variable for readable body/UI. Headings are uppercase with tight tracking; labels are 11–12 px with wide tracking.

## Layout and components
1240 px content width, 1480 px cinematic width, 80–120 px desktop section rhythm. Asymmetric hero, technical 1 px grid/borders, square corners, controlled whitespace. Orange primary buttons; transparent secondary buttons; 48 px minimum controls.

## Motion
GSAP reveal and selected parallax only. 450–700 ms transitions, no novelty cursor, no autoplay audio. Disable non-essential animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`; reduce intensity on mobile.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic landmarks/headings, visible teal focus, persistent form labels, aria-live form status, 44 px touch targets and complete content without animation.

## Media policy
All current industrial imagery is newly generated for this new project and is representative. Replace it with approved real factory, product, inspection, warehouse and packing photography before claims-based launch.
